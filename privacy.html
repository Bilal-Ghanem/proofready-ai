Privacy notice

Validation-build version · 4 September 2026

This public validation build has no account system, server-side database, contact form, advertising tracker or product analytics.

Workspace data

Information entered into the ProofReady workspace is stored in your browser's local storage. The app does not intentionally transmit that workspace data to ProofReady. Data you export, print, sync through your browser, or back up through your device is handled by you and the relevant browser or device provider.

Hosting

The static site host may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details, requested files and timestamps for security and service operation. Review the host's privacy documentation before relying on this notice for a live deployment.

Important limitations

Browser-local storage is not an enterprise backup, retention, encryption or access-control system. Do not enter information that your organisation has not approved for storage on the device and browser you use.

Contact and updates

A dedicated ProofReady contact address will be added before research outreach begins. This notice must be updated if forms, analytics, cloud sync, payments or accounts are introduced.
