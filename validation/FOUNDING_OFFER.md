# ProofReady AI — founding-customer offer

**Status:** private validation proposal; not available for sale until launch approvals are complete.

## Beachhead

Owner, operations, compliance or HR lead at a 10–100-person AI/digital agency or small consultancy that already uses AI across multiple roles and currently coordinates inventory, guidance and records across spreadsheets, documents or an LMS.

## Core promise

Build a coherent AI-literacy working file from the systems and roles your team actually has—in under 60 minutes, without a new cloud account.

## Paid pilot offers

### Evidence Pack — €59 one time

- Local ProofReady workspace
- Unlimited AI uses and role groups
- Suggested role/context topics
- Controls checklist and policy starter
- JSON and print/PDF export
- 12 months of template updates if the product launches

### Guided Setup — €249 one time

- Everything in Evidence Pack

## Boundaries

- Not legal advice, legal classification, certification or a compliance guarantee
- Does not provide the training itself
- Does not cover high-risk-system conformity obligations
- Buyer remains responsible for validating records with appropriate internal and professional owners
- Local browser storage is not enterprise backup or access control

## Discovery call structure — 15 minutes

1. **Current state (4 min):** “Walk me through the last time a client, board member or colleague asked what your team does about AI literacy.”
2. **Artefacts (3 min):** “Where do the tool list, role context, guidance and proof live today?”
3. **Friction (3 min):** “What is slow, incomplete or hard to review?”
4. **Prototype (3 min):** show inventory → roles → evidence pack; do not explain price first.
5. **Commitment (2 min):** “Would this be materially better than your current method? Why?” Then test €59 and €249 without discounting.

## Evidence to record

- Exact language used for the problem
- Existing substitute and time spent
- Trigger event and urgency
- Which output creates value
- Objections or missing trust signal
- Offer selected, rejected or deferred—and why
- Written paid-pilot commitment, if voluntarily given

## Go/no-go gate

Continue beyond concierge validation only after ten qualified conversations produce at least three independently stated coordination problems, three written commitments at €59+, and either one guided-setup commitment or one consultant expecting reuse across three clients. Stop or pivot if fewer than two paid commitments emerge.
