# ProofReady AI — validation economics

**Model date / price check:** 4 September 2026
**Currency:** euro unless marked otherwise. Prices exclude VAT and income/corporate tax.
**Decision use:** planning model, not a guarantee. No spend, account creation, deployment or outreach has occurred.

## Executive conclusion

Start with the **€0 validation plan**. The product already works locally; the next uncertainty is willingness to pay, not infrastructure. A domain and brand mailbox are the only credible early cash needs, and even those should wait until approved interviews require a public identity. Do not release the optional budget until at least three written paid commitments.

At the stated order scenarios, cumulative revenue at 12 months ranges from **€721 to €18,245** and contribution after payment fees and fulfilment time ranges from **€557 to €13,654**, before fixed launch spend, acquisition labour, VAT and income/corporate tax. The base case is €4,745 revenue and €3,589 contribution. These are scenario outputs—not historical performance, a probability-weighted expectation, or a promise.

## 1. What is genuinely necessary

Necessary for validation:

1. the existing local MVP and evidence-pack files;
2. a list of qualified buyers and a spreadsheet/CSV for measurement;
3. a brand-controlled reply route before any real outreach;
4. clear scope, privacy and non-legal-advice wording;
5. payment processing only after a buyer agrees to pay.

Not necessary yet: SaaS hosting, a backend/database, AI API usage, CRM, paid analytics, transcription, LMS/course platform, broad market reports, paid social, or automated tax software. None resolves the central uncertainty: whether buyers value the joined evidence workflow enough to pay.

## 3. Unit economics

### Planning assumptions

- Evidence Pack: **€59 one-time**, 15 minutes support at €40/hour = **€10 labour**.
- Guided Setup: **€249 one-time**, 45-minute session + 45 minutes preparation/review = **1.5 hours = €60 labour**.
- Payment fee: conservative blended **3.0% + €0.30/order**. This is an assumption, not a processor quote; it accommodates a mixed card base but not FX, disputes or refunds.
- Marginal hosting/software: €0 during validation.

Formula:
`contribution = gross customer payment − (3% × payment + €0.30) − fulfilment hours × €40`

| Offer | Gross price | Payment fee | Founder fulfilment | Cash after fee | Economic contribution | Contribution margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| €59 Evidence Pack | €59.00 | €2.07 | €10.00 | €56.93 | **€46.93** | **79.5%** |
| €249 Guided Setup | €249.00 | €7.77 | €60.00 | €241.23 | **€181.23** | **72.8%** |

The €249 offer contributes more euros but is not “free margin”: delivery capacity is about 26 guided setups per 40-hour week before sales/admin, and quality may degrade earlier.

### VAT and tax caveats

- Decide whether public prices are VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive before selling. The Evidence Pack may be treated as an electronically supplied service; Guided Setup may be classified differently because it has material human delivery. Classification and place-of-supply are fact- and country-specific.
- For eligible cross-border EU B2B sales, reverse charge may apply only with correct customer status/VAT-number validation and invoicing. EU B2C sales may create destination-VAT/OSS obligations. Domestic registration thresholds and small-business regimes vary.
- Payment fees are normally charged on the customer payment, including VAT. If €59/€249 are VAT-inclusive at 20%, VAT embedded is €9.83/€41.50. Contribution then falls to approximately **€37.10 / €139.73** before income/corporate tax.
- Refunds, disputes, accounting, FX and income/corporate tax are excluded. Keep a **5% of revenue contingency** until actual data exists; one €20 dispute is material against a €59 order.

The main tables do **not** price prospect research/outreach or sales calls. At five minutes per qualified contact, 30 minutes per call, and €40/hour, steady monthly acquisition labour would be approximately:

| Upside | 13.3 | 15.6 | 7.8 | €845 | €69 | €94 | **€25** |

`*` Conservative weighted contribution uses the 85/15 offer-mix assumption; base 75/25; upside 65/35.

This does not mean “do not validate”: founder selling time is normal discovery cost. It means low-volume personalised outbound is not yet a scalable profit channel. Profitability needs faster research, warm/referral/consultant distribution, higher guided/bundle value, or better conversion. At base month 12, €503 contribution less roughly €453 steady acquisition labour leaves only about **€50 before the €10 mailbox, VAT and tax**.

## 7. Break-even and cash needed

Using fulfilment contribution:

- Lean 3-month **€120 cap**: `€120/€46.93 = 2.56` → **3 packs**, or `€120/€181.23 = 0.66` → **1 guided setup**.
- Lean **€50 committed spend**: **2 packs** or **1 guided setup**.
- Optional **€1,200 cap**: `€1,200/€46.93 = 25.57` → **26 packs**; `€1,200/€181.23 = 6.62` → **7 guided setups**.
- Base 75/25 mix average contribution: `0.75×€46.93 + 0.25×€181.23 = €80.505`; optional cap break-even `€1,200/€80.505 = 14.91` → **15 orders**.

Cash requirement:

- **Recommended validation:** €0.
- **Lean committed path:** €50 over three months; hold up to €120 only if approved corrections arise.
- **Optional path:** up to €1,200 after gates; €1,290 through month 12 if mailbox continues.
- Keep tax/VAT collected out of operating cash and maintain a refund/dispute buffer. A prudent minimum after taking payments is the larger of one guided refund (**€249**) or estimated VAT/tax payable; exact reserve requires seller-country advice.

Break-even by timing under the stated order schedule: lean spend to date is recovered in month 3 in base/upside and month 5 in conservative (€93.86 cumulative contribution versus €70 cumulative domain/mailbox spend). The full optional cap is recovered during month 6 in base on fulfilment contribution, but not after acquisition labour; conservative does not recover it within 12 months.

## 9. Objective range, gates and stop rules

There is not enough evidence to assign defensible probabilities, so reporting a probability-weighted “expected revenue” would be false precision. The objective planning envelope is:

- **3 months:** €59–€2,202 cumulative revenue; **€43.98–€1,540.44** contribution after the 5% contingency, before fixed spend/acquisition labour/tax.
- **6 months:** €426–€6,855 revenue; **€300.72–€4,790.10** contribution on the same basis.
- **12 months:** €721–€18,245 revenue; **€520.62–€12,741.90** contribution on the same basis.
- A more mechanically grounded downside is roughly **six conservative orders/year**, not nine; zero orders remains possible until paid intent is demonstrated.

Thirty-day decision gate after 10 qualified conversations:

- continue only if ≥3 independently describe the joined inventory→roles→literacy action/evidence problem;
- ≥3 make a paid-pilot or written price commitment at ≥€59;
- ≥1 wants guided setup or a consultant expects reuse across ≥3 clients;
- stop/pivot if fewer than two paid commitments result.

If a €200 search test is later approved: cap at €10/day and review at €50; stop with no qualified lead by €75, qualified-lead cost above €25, or <5% visit→qualified lead after 100 relevant clicks. At 25% lead→paid, €25/lead implies €100 cash CAC—already above the pack’s €46.93 fulfilment contribution.

**Bottom line:** validate at €0, release lean identity spend only when interviews justify it, and treat every revenue/profit figure here as a falsifiable forecast—not a guarantee.
