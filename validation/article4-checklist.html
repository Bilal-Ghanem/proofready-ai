AI LITERACY EVIDENCE CHECKLIST
PRIVATE VALIDATION EDITION · SEP 2026

Can your team explain its AI-literacy approach?

Use this 15-minute checklist to find missing operational evidence. It follows the practical themes in the European Commission's AI Literacy Q&A: systems and organisational role, people's knowledge and experience, use context, proportionate actions, and internal records.

Important: This is an operational planning aid, not legal advice or a compliance assessment. Article 4 does not prescribe one certificate, training format, officer or governance structure.
01

Know the AI use

We maintain a list of material AI systems and AI-enabled features used for work.Inventory
For each system, we record the business purpose and whether we act as provider or deployer.Role & purpose
We record relevant data, output, impact, failure and human-oversight context.Use context
Each material use has an accountable owner and a review date.Ownership
02

Understand the people

We identify employees, contractors and other people operating or using AI on our behalf.Population
We consider their technical knowledge, experience, education and existing training.Baseline
Managers, technical teams and business users receive different guidance where context differs.Role fit
03

Choose proportionate actions

Our actions cover AI basics plus the systems, limitations and risks people actually encounter.Content
People know approved-use, confidentiality, verification and escalation expectations.Guidance
Higher-impact uses receive stronger role preparation and human-review expectations.Proportionality
We chose the format—guidance, briefing, exercise or course—based on the real need.Format
04

Keep a reviewable record

We record the selected literacy action, audience, status, date and evidence reference.Evidence
Our acceptable-use rules, incident path and owner are visible to relevant people.Controls
We revisit the inventory and literacy needs when systems, roles or risks change.Review
Templates and source guidance are dated or versioned.Versioning

Interpretation

0–4 checked: establish ownership and inventory first.   5–10: connect existing practices into one working file.   11–15: review gaps, evidence quality and change triggers. A higher count shows document completeness, not legal compliance.
