## Why now—without alarmism

Article 4 of the EU AI Act has applied since 2 February 2025 and national supervision is now active. The current rule asks AI providers and deployers to support literacy in a way that reflects people's knowledge and the systems' use context. It does not prescribe one course, certificate or governance structure.

Source: European Commission AI Literacy Q&A, reviewed 4 September 2026.

## What the pack connects

1. **AI-use inventory** — purpose, organisation role, owner, impact, data context and human oversight.
2. **People and context** — roles, current experience and relevant systems.
3. **Literacy actions** — suggested topics, chosen activity, status, date and evidence reference.
4. **Programme controls** — policy, incident path, owner and review cadence.
5. **Reviewable export** — structured JSON and print/PDF output with template version and source links.

## FAQ

**Does Article 4 require a certificate?**  
The Commission says no specific certificate is required and internal records of training and/or other guidance may be kept.

**Does completing this pack make us compliant?**  
No. It organises an operational working file. Scope, sufficiency and related AI Act duties depend on facts and applicable national law.

**Does company information leave the device?**  
The current app has no backend. Data is stored in this browser. Exporting, printing, browser sync, device backups and any later form are separate user/provider data flows.

**Is training included?**  
No. The app helps choose and record context-appropriate actions. Use qualified internal or external training where needed.

**What if we use a high-risk AI system?**  
Use qualified support. High-risk systems can carry additional duties that this narrow pack does not cover.
