# ProofReady AI — September 2026 validation report

**Evidence cut-off:** 4 September 2026

**Verdict:** **Conditional GO for a 30-day concierge validation; NO-GO for SaaS build-out, paid acquisition or broad governance features.**

**Product:** a local-browser workflow for organising AI uses, organisational role, staff context, literacy actions, controls and review records. It does not provide training, legal advice, certification or a compliance determination.

This is the executive index. The sourced analysis and assumptions are in `validation/`.

## Decision in one page

Article 4 of the EU AI Act has applied since 2 February 2025. The current Commission guidance says providers and deployers must support AI literacy among people using AI on their behalf, taking account of knowledge, experience, training and use context. It does not prescribe one course, certificate, AI officer or governance structure. National supervision is active, but authority, penalty and territorial details are fact- and country-specific.

That supports a real operational problem but not a proven business. Eurostat's 2025 data reports AI use by 20.0% of EU enterprises with 10+ employees. Combining official size-band adoption rates with 2023 enterprise counts produces a broad estimate of approximately **342,755 AI-using EU enterprises with 10–249 staff**. A narrower professional/scientific/technical proxy is approximately **25,115**. Neither number measures intent to buy.

The primary competitor is not an enterprise governance suite; it is **spreadsheet + policy template + existing LMS** at little incremental cost. trail already combines registry and literacy features, dedicated Article 4 training vendors serve certificate-oriented demand, and free official guidance and open-source projects constrain information/template pricing. ProofReady wins only if buyers value a joined, local-first working file enough to pay.

## Beachhead and offer to test

Prioritise:

1. 10–100-person AI/digital agencies with a client questionnaire, AI-service launch or policy refresh.
2. Small AI-governance, privacy, DPO or HR consultancies that could reuse the workflow across clients.
3. Professional-services teams only where there is an identifiable trigger and no mature joined GRC/LMS workflow.

Price tests:

- **€59 one-time Evidence Pack**
- **€249 one-time Guided Setup**

These are validation prices, not a live purchasing offer. VAT, terms, payment handling, support scope and public claims require review before launch.

## 30-day gate

Proceed only after **10 qualified conversations** if all are true:

- at least 3 independently describe the inventory → roles → literacy action/evidence coordination problem before seeing the product;
- at least 3 give a paid-pilot or written price commitment at ≥€59;
- at least one wants a €249 guided setup or one consultant expects reuse across ≥3 clients;
- at least 4 say the joined workflow is materially better than their current spreadsheet/LMS process;
- the disclaimer prevents repeated certification/compliance misunderstanding.

**Stop or pivot** if fewer than two paid commitments result. If buyers mainly want training, partner or refer rather than build a course. If consultants show the strongest pull, prioritise separate local client packs, import and versioning.

## Economics summary

Cumulative order hypotheses at 3 / 6 / 12 months:

- Conservative: **1 / 4 / 9**
- Base: **6 / 18 / 45**
- Upside: **18 / 55 / 145**

Base economic profit before fixed launch budget is modelled at approximately **€550 / €1,382 / €3,589**, after assumed payment fees and founder delivery time at €40/hour. A 5% revenue contingency lowers those values to about **€513 / €1,291 / €3,352**. These are explicit scenarios, not forecasts.

Start at **€0 cash**. A lean three-month cap of €120 is justified only after interviews begin; an optional €1,200 evidence-building budget remains gated behind ≥3 written paid commitments. See `validation/ECONOMICS.md` for formulas, offer mix, unit economics, break-even and channel stop rules.
